Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit our website.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Scope of this policy

This policy describes the Fusion Health Juice Bar website. Ordering and payment take place on external delivery platforms, which have their own privacy policies.

Information you choose to share

If you contact the store, you may share your name, contact details, and information about your question or order. That information helps us respond to your request. Please avoid including sensitive medical or payment information in a general inquiry.

Browser storage

This website uses local storage on your device to remember whether the welcome video has been shown and to cache the ingredient spotlight. The ingredient cache is refreshed after 24 hours; the welcome preference remains until browser storage is cleared. You can remove this data through your browser settings. Clearing it may cause the welcome video to appear again.

Embedded content and external services

The home page includes Instagram content, and the location page includes a Google Maps embed. When those features load, the providers may receive your IP address, browser information, and information about the page you visited, and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. Your browser settings can limit third-party cookies or block embedded content.

Links to DoorDash, Uber Eats, and social media take you to services operated by other companies. Information you provide there, including account, delivery, and payment details, is handled under those services’ policies. Review their policies before sharing information.

Technical information

Website hosting services may process standard request information, such as IP addresses, requested pages, browser details, and timestamps, to deliver pages and maintain service security.

Your questions and choices

To ask about information you have shared directly with Fusion Health Juice Bar, including access, correction, deletion, or retention, contact the store. Requests about a delivery or social media account should also be directed to the provider that holds that account.

Call (860) 461-0575 or write to Fusion Health Juice Bar, 879 New Britain Ave, Hartford, CT 06106. Visit our contact page for store details and hours.

Policy updates

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
