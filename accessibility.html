Accessibility

Help accessing our menu, store information, and online services.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Access to our information

We want everyone to be able to find our menu, hours, and location. If a part of this website is difficult to use, please contact the store for help with the information you need.

Report an accessibility barrier

Call (860) 461-0575 during store hours, or write to Fusion Health Juice Bar, 879 New Britain Ave, Hartford, CT 06106. You can also visit us in person for help with menu and store information.

When reporting a website issue, please include the page address, what you were trying to do, and what prevented you from doing it. If helpful, include your browser, device, or assistive technology. Please let us know how you prefer to be contacted.

Using this site

You can use your browser’s zoom controls to enlarge text and the Tab key to move between links and buttons. The welcome video includes a Skip video button that closes the popup. These policy pages also include a Skip to content link at the start of the page.

Third-party features

Instagram content, Google Maps, DoorDash, and Uber Eats are provided by other companies. Their accessibility may differ from this website. If a feature prevents you from finding our menu, address, or hours, call the store for assistance.

Accessibility status

This statement provides a way to request help and report barriers. It does not certify that every page or external service meets a particular accessibility standard.
